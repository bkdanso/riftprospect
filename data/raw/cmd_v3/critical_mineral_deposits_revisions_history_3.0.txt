Revision 3.0 - September, 2026:
____________________________

The deposit table was updated in 2026 (ver. 3) to include 592 new deposits in addition to updating 248 previous entries. Critical minerals have been updated to include those on the 2025 U.S. critical minerals list.

2025 critical minerals list - https://www.usgs.gov/news/science-snippet/interior-department-releases-final-2025-list-critical-minerals.
