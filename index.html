Where might the next Eagle be? A public prospectivity map of the Midcontinent Rift

Ranks every 200 m square of the 2025 USGS airborne survey of Michigan's Upper Peninsula and northern Wisconsin by how closely its magnetic and radiometric signature resembles a small mafic-ultramafic intrusion, the host of magmatic nickel-copper-cobalt-PGE deposits. A higher score means better odds, not a discovery.
